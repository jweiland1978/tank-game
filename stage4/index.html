How to Play

Goal

Be the last tank standing to win the round. After the match, the most round wins takes the standings. Ties break on cash, then damage.

Turns

Wind changes, you aim, you fire, then everyone waits. The turn ends when the shell, the fire, and the falling dirt are finished.

Aiming

Drag on the battlefield to set the barrel and the power. A− and A+ nudge the angle. P− and P+ nudge the power.

Keyboard: arrow keys change angle and power in bigger steps. Hold Shift for fine steps. Space fires. Tab, Q, or E switches weapon. A and D drive, if you have fuel.

Wind

The wind chip shows which way the air pushes, and how hard. A strong wind bends long shots. The laser sight draws that curve.

Money and the store

Hits, kills, round wins, and surviving pay cash. Leftover cash earns a little interest. Between rounds, buy weapons, accessories, and upgrades. Sell gives back half of one round. Basic Shell is free and never runs out.
