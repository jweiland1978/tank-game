# Boom Ridge

Stages 1–3 below still show the earlier working title, Iron Rig.

A turn-based artillery tank game for the browser. Set your angle and power, read the wind, and fire across hills that blow apart. 2 to 4 tanks, each human or computer (Easy, Normal, Hard). Pass the device for local play.

**These are test builds.** It's not finished.

- **Stage 4 (newest):** https://jweiland1978.github.io/tank-game/stage4/index.html?v=3 — computer tanks now fire the weapons they buy (napalm, nukes, MIRVs and more) and avoid blowing themselves up, computer aim retuned so Easy and Normal take several shots to find their range (and wind throws them off), a new title screen with Settings and How to Play, a richer pass-the-device screen, and 130+ taunts that stay clear of name labels.
- **Stage 3:** https://jweiland1978.github.io/tank-game/stage3/index.html?v=1 — deadlier napalm, tank taunts, three new weapons (Popcorn Bomb, Dirt Magnet, Lightning Rod), richer sound with a volume control, bigger touch controls with fine aim/power nudges, a pass-the-device screen for hot-seat play, and a store that fits on a phone.
- **Stage 2:** https://jweiland1978.github.io/tank-game/stage2/index.html?v=1 — money for hits, kills and wins, a store between rounds (napalm, nuke, roller, MIRV, funky bomb and more), shields, parachutes, fuel, laser sight, armor and barrel upgrades, multi-round matches with final standings, and five terrain styles (rolling hills, mountains, canyon, islands, spires).
- **Stage 1:** https://jweiland1978.github.io/tank-game/stage1/index.html?v=1 — the core game: destructible hills, wind, 2 to 4 tanks, computer players.

Works offline after loading, with no ads, tracking or outside requests. Sounds are generated in the browser.
